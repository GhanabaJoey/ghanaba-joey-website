# Ghanaba Joey — Brand Reference

## Identity

**GHANABA JOEY**  
Creator • Host • Entrepreneur

## Core positioning

> Creating experiences. Building community. Creating opportunities.

## Brand pillars

- **CREATE** — experiences, content, live moments
- **TEACH** — education and guidance for creators
- **CONNECT** — community and relationships
- **BUILD** — opportunities, products, and partnerships

## Visual direction (target)

- Dark cinematic
- Metallic gold
- Glass
- Futuristic 3D (selective, premium)
- Luxury editorial typography and layout

## Ecosystem distinctions

These must remain **separate** in messaging and information architecture:

### Destiny Helper

- Part of the daily **Ghanaba Joey LIVE**
- Daily at 2PM UK (when promoted on-site)
- Community-driven LIVE experience: competition, connections, rewards/blessings
- **Not** the same product as Official Monthly Box Games

### Official Monthly Box Games

- Separate structured **monthly** event
- Dedicated registration/application experience on GhanabaJoey.com (`/boxgames` today)
- Distinct identity and information from Destiny Helper

### NextWave Creator Network

- Separate creator network/business
- Connected to Ghanaba Joey’s ecosystem
- Primary destination: [nextwavecreatornetwork.com](https://nextwavecreatornetwork.com)
- The personal site must **not** become a duplicate of the NextWave website

### Website Development

- Commercial service offered through the personal brand
- Future lead-generation surface on GhanabaJoey.com

### Advertise With Me

- Commercial partnership opportunity (sponsored content, LIVE, TikTok, events, etc.)
- Future lead-generation surface on GhanabaJoey.com
